Every Blood Group Matters

Discover something interesting about each blood group.

A+

A+ is one of the most common blood groups, so A+ donors are especially valuable for maintaining blood supplies.

O−

O− red blood cells are commonly known as the “universal red-cell donor”.

AB+

AB+ individuals have both A and B antigens as well as the Rh(D) antigen on their red blood cells.

B−

B− is one of the rarer blood groups.
B− individuals can receive red blood cells from B− and O− donors.

O+

O+ is one of the most common blood groups in the world, making O+ donors extremely important.

A−

A− red blood cells can be given to A−, A+, AB− and AB+ recipients.

AB−

AB− is one of the rarest of the eight major blood groups, making compatible donors particularly valuable.

B+

B+ is among the more common blood groups, so its donors can play a major role in keeping hospitals supplied.
